Biyaheng Batangan • Taal, Batangas

Taal Heritage Escapade: A walk through the heritage town

Step back in time in Taal’s preserved streets: a Spanish-era basilica, historic ancestral-house museums, vintage cameras, and a 125-step climb to a pilgrimage shrine.

Duration5 hrs 50 min
Stops5 destinations
Tour daysTuesday to Sunday
Starts8:30 AM at Taal Basilica front plaza, Taal

The five stops

Each place was chosen for its story and for how well it fits together on one route.

1

Taal Basilica

Town center • Free entry

The Spanish-era basilica that anchors the heritage town, known for its belfry and large bell. Renovation work may limit some areas.

2

Leon Apacible Museum

Heritage town • Tue – Sun, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM • Free

A heritage ancestral house museum managed by the National Historical Commission of the Philippines.

3

Marcela Agoncillo Museum

Calle Marcela Agoncillo • Tue – Sun, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM • Free

The birthplace of Marcela Agoncillo, where you can see flags of the Philippine revolution.

4

Galleria Taal

Heritage town • Tue – Sun, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM • ₱100

A heritage-home museum of old Philippine photographs and vintage cameras. Try kapeng barako and tablea tsokolate at the backyard café.

5

Shrine of Our Lady of Caysasay

North of the town center • Free entry

A pilgrimage site reached by a flight of 125 steps. Saint Lucia’s Well sits along the way.
