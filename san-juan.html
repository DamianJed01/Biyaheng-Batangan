Biyaheng Batangan • San Juan, Batangas

Laiya Escapade: Laiya’s coves and beaches

Mix culture with sea and sun: a 19th-century church, the local market, the public beach in Laiya, a boat tour of coves and a snorkeling site, and an adventure park.

Duration9 hrs
Stops5 destinations
Tour daysDaily, weather permitting
Starts7:30 AM at San Juan Nepomuceno Parish Church, San Juan

The five stops

Each place was chosen for its story and for how well it fits together on one route.

1

San Juan Nepomuceno Parish Church

Town center • Free entry

A 19th-century Baroque church in the town center, declared a historical landmark by the National Historical Commission of the Philippines.

2

San Juan Public Market

Town center • About ₱100 for breakfast

Join the locals for breakfast and pick up fresh fruit and snacks for the beach.

3

Hugom Public Beach

Laiya • Ecological fee ₱50 per person

The public stretch of Laiya’s shoreline. Swim, relax, and take photos. The ₱50 ecological fee is paid at the tourism reception area.

4

Beach-hopping boat tour

Laiya • Boat shared by up to 10 guests

A guided boat trip with three stops: two coves and a snorkeling site. Life vests are provided.

5

Laiya Adventure Park

Laiya • About ₱350

End the day with active fun: obstacle courses, zip lines, and a climbing wall.
