Batangas City

Explore the provincial capital’s historic center, then head up to Montemaria Shrine for sweeping views of Batangas Bay and a walk on its famous glass bridge.

5 stops • 7 hrs 25 min • Tuesday to Friday

Taal

A walk through the heritage town

Step back in time in Taal’s preserved streets: a Spanish-era basilica, historic ancestral-house museums, vintage cameras, and a 125-step climb to a pilgrimage shrine.

5 stops • 5 hrs 50 min • Tuesday to Sunday

Public tour: ₱790 per person Private tour: from ₱880 per person

Explore Taal

San Juan

Laiya’s coves and beaches

Mix culture with sea and sun: a 19th-century church, the local market, the public beach in Laiya, a boat tour of coves and a snorkeling site, and an adventure park.

5 stops • 9 hrs • Daily, weather permitting

Public tour: ₱2,020 per person Private tour: from ₱2,100 per person

Nasugbu

Gardens and island coves

Begin in the calm gardens of Caleruega, then sail from Calayo Beach to Pulo Island and two quiet coves for a full day on the water.

5 stops • 8 hrs 25 min • Daily, weather permitting
