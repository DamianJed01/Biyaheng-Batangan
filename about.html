About Us

Local travel, made easy, from the people who call Batangas home.

Who we are

Biyaheng Batangan is a travel and tour business that creates day trips across the province of Batangas. We handpick five destinations, Lipa, Batangas City, Taal, San Juan, and Nasugbu, and plan each tour stop by stop, with realistic travel times and clear prices.

Whether you join a public group tour or book a private tour for your family, friends, or class, our goal is the same: a relaxed, well-timed day that shows you the best of Batangas.
