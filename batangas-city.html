Biyaheng Batangan • Batangas City, Batangas

Batangas City Escapade: Bay, basilica, and the Miracle Walk

Explore the provincial capital’s historic center, then head up to Montemaria Shrine for sweeping views of Batangas Bay and a walk on its famous glass bridge.

Duration7 hrs 25 min
Stops5 destinations
Tour daysTuesday to Friday
Starts8:00 AM at Plaza Mabini, Batangas City

The five stops

Each place was chosen for its story and for how well it fits together on one route.

1

Basilica of the Immaculate Conception

City center • Free entry

A Baroque-style church in the heart of Batangas City, with roots going back to the late 16th century.

2

Plaza Mabini

City center • Free

A shaded central plaza with a statue of national hero Apolinario Mabini. A good spot for a first group photo.

3

Museo ng Batangas

City center • About ₱30

A museum in a historic building with exhibits on the region’s history, traditional attire, art, and crafts.

4

Montemaria Shrine

Brgy. Pagkilatan • Grounds free • Miracle Glass Walk ₱300

A hilltop pilgrimage site overlooking Batangas Bay, known for its large Virgin Mary statue. Your ticket includes the Miracle Glass Walk.

5

Batangas Bay Walk

Along Batangas Bay • Free, snacks extra

A paved coastal promenade along Batangas Bay, popular for relaxed strolls. End the day with street snacks by the water.
