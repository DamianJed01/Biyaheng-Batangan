Biyaheng Batangan • Lipa, Batangas

Lipa Escapade: Coffee, heritage, and honey

Start with kapeng barako, visit the city’s landmark church and a Spanish-era heritage house, pause at Carmel, then end the day at a working bee farm.

Duration7 hrs 40 min
Stops5 destinations
Tour daysTuesday to Friday
Starts8:00 AM at Café de Lipa, San Carlos Drive, Mataas na Lupa

The five stops

Each place was chosen for its story and for how well it fits together on one route.

1

Café de Lipa

San Carlos Drive, Mataas na Lupa

Start the day where Lipa’s coffee story is told. Taste kapeng barako, the strong Liberica coffee that made Batangas famous, with traditional suman.

2

San Sebastian Cathedral

C.M. Recto Ave. • Open daily, 5:00 AM – 7:00 PM • Free entry

The landmark church at the heart of the city and the seat of the Archdiocese of Lipa. A quiet first look at the city’s religious and cultural life.

3

Casa de Segunda

G.A. Solis St. • Mon – Fri, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM • ₱50

A heritage house that gives a glimpse of the elegant way of life in Lipa during the Spanish colonial period.

4

Monastery of Our Lady of Mt. Carmel

P. Torres St. • Free entry (donations welcome)

Known simply as “Carmel,” this is a peaceful spiritual oasis in the middle of the busy city, visited by pilgrims from across the country.

5

Milea Bee Farm

Kurba Rd. • Guided tour about 1.5 hours • ₱200

A working farm, not a resort. Learn how bees live, taste honey straight from the hive, and enjoy honey drinks and a light snack. Hard to reach by public transport, which is why our van makes it easy.
