Biyaheng Batangan • Nasugbu, Batangas

Nasugbu Escapade: Gardens and island coves

Begin in the calm gardens of Caleruega, then sail from Calayo Beach to Pulo Island and two quiet coves for a full day on the water.

Duration8 hrs 25 min
Stops5 destinations
Tour daysDaily, weather permitting
Starts8:00 AM at Caleruega Church parking area, Nasugbu

The five stops

Each place was chosen for its story and for how well it fits together on one route.

1

Caleruega Church and Gardens

Open daily, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM • ₱30 entrance

A peaceful retreat of gardens, trees, a hanging bridge, and a hillside church. Expect plenty of walking and stairs.

2

Calayo Beach

Calayo • Environmental fee ₱20

Our boarding point for the island-hopping trip. The boat is shared by up to 10 guests.

3

Pulo Island

Entrance ₱25

A small island with clear water for swimming and snorkeling.

4

Bituin Cove

Entrance ₱25

A secluded cove with calm water, perfect for swimming and a relaxed lunch.

5

Kayraang Cove

No entrance fee

A small cove where boats can only bring passengers who stay in the water. Bring a life vest and enjoy a final swim.
